# Successful UK Aesthetic Clinic Ad Patterns — Research Notes

**Date:** 20 May 2026
**Researcher:** Mark + Claude
**Purpose:** Identify what's working in long-running paid social for UK aesthetic clinics, to inform the Eskeen Clinic CO2 laser ad refresh.

Method: Facebook Ad Library, filtered by **Active** + UK, searching across "CO2 laser", "skin laser", "skin rejuvenation clinic", then drilling into advertisers with multiple variants and/or ads running 3+ months (signal: they're spending real money and have likely converted).

## The headline find

**Thérapie Clinic UK** — national chain — **16 active ad variants** running concurrently right now. Their evergreen cosmetic-injection ad has been running across multiple creatives for 2+ months. Single biggest paid-social spender in this category in the UK.

**Lavelle Clinic** (Manchester) — independent — has had **the same celebrity-testimonial ad running since 25 September 2024 = 20 MONTHS LIVE.** Anything that runs for 20 months is, by definition, profitable.

Those two are the templates worth stealing.

## Pattern 1 — Thérapie Clinic UK (national chain, 16 active variants)

### 1A — Evergreen "Every treatment is personal" (7 variants, max 10 weeks old)

> Every treatment is personal. Discover more about our cosmetic injections and book a free consultation.

**Why it works:** One sentence. No price. No urgency. Just identity + invitation. Probably their highest-ROAS cold-prospecting ad. Run as image carousels with different patient photos so the *creative* refreshes while the *copy stays winning*.

### 1B — Emotional+authority (5 variants, 5 days old, fast scaling)

> Imagine never worrying about shaving again 😍
> Laser hair removal at Thérapie Clinic delivers long-lasting smooth skin with expert care and advanced technology.
> The UK's laser hair removal experts.

**Why it works:**
- Hook = imagined future state ("never worrying about…")
- Benefit + credibility one-liner
- Authority claim: **"The UK's [category] experts"** ← strongest copy weapon they use

### 1C — Promotional/concrete-saving

> The life hack you've been waiting for: laser smooth skin that lasts. Save up to 30% on our best-selling laser hair removal bundles.
> CTA: **Ready for Maintenance-Free Mornings?**

**Why it works:**
- Concrete % saving beats £-saving for memorability
- Question-style CTA ("Ready for X?") beats "Book Now"

### 1D — Pain-led re-engagement

> Don't live with a tattoo you no longer love. Our advanced lasers target ink effectively for clear results.
> CTA: **Ready for a Clean Slate?**

**Why it works:** Negative-state framing ("don't live with…") + clean question CTA.

## Pattern 2 — Lavelle Clinic (Manchester independent, 20-month winner)

### 2A — Celebrity testimonial (live since 25 Sep 2024 — 20 months)

> We had the pleasure to have @faryalmakhdoom in our Manchester clinic again 🤩
>
> This time for a skin analysis consultation using the Observe 360 👀 taking a look deeper into the skin and working out a skin treatment plan.
>
> Faryal opted for the Harmony XL pro ClearSkin Rejuvenation treatment.
>
> This treatment is virtually pain free, minimal down time, and treats some acne scarring, tightens skin, closes pores and increases collagen production!
>
> DM 📲 for more information or book a free consultation online 🤍

**Why it works (and why it's the most copyable template for Eskeen):**
- Named client (Faryal Makhdoom = real-name social proof, looks like organic content)
- Branded tech ("Observe 360", "Harmony XL Pro") — sounds medical, not beauty
- Treatment named with brand confidence ("ClearSkin Rejuvenation") — *not* "CO2 laser"
- Benefit stack: pain-free, downtime, scarring, tightening, pores, collagen — six benefits in 20 words
- Soft CTA: "DM or book a free consultation" — lower-friction than "book now"

### 2B — Non-celebrity client testimonial (12 months running)

Same template as 2A, but client is "our beautiful client Monet". Confirms 2A's pattern works without paying for influencer access.

### 2C — Date-windowed promotion (current)

> Eid Glow pending and we have a mix for you ladies!
>
> Head to toe laser (6) and 2 free Hydrafacial's £1800
> 3 or more areas of laser 40% off and 1 free Hydrafacial
> Hydrafacial £72 each
>
> For bookings made from 4th May until 1st June 2026 only.
> £50 deposit required to book.

**Why it works:**
- **Cultural-moment hook** ("Eid Glow") — outperforms generic "this month only"
- **Tiered offer** (premium bundle / mid bundle / single-service) gives every budget a yes
- **Hard date window** ("4th May until 1st June") feels concrete vs "May only"
- **£50 deposit gate** — filters out low-intent clicks; protects calendar; lifts show-rate

## Common ingredients across long-runners

| Ingredient | Used by | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Authority claim** ("UK's X experts", "Finest Medi-Spa", named credentials) | Therapie, SoKo, Calm & Gentle | Defends premium price |
| **Brevity for cold** (1–3 sentences) | Therapie | Cold audiences don't read paragraphs |
| **Long-form for testimonials only** | Lavelle | Stories sustain attention; offers don't |
| **Named tech/treatment** ("Observe 360", "Harmony XL Pro") | Lavelle | Premium signal; "CO2 laser" alone sounds generic |
| **Question-style CTA** ("Ready for X?") | Therapie | Mental commitment > directive |
| **Date-windowed offers** | Lavelle | Concrete > "this month" |
| **Deposit-to-book gate** | Lavelle | Filters bot/curious clicks |
| **Cultural-moment hooks** ("Eid Glow") | Lavelle | Outperforms generic urgency |
| **Six-benefit stack in ≤20 words** | Lavelle | Maximises perceived value per word |
| **DM-as-CTA option** | Lavelle, SoKo | Lower-friction than form |

## What's missing from current Eskeen ad (current 250-word version)

1. **Too long.** Lavelle's 20-month winner is 80 words. Therapie's evergreen is **15 words**. Eskeen's current ad is over 250 words and buries the offer in paragraph 6.
2. **No authority anchor.** "Nurse Led Clinic" buried as line 1 without naming Elanda/Mavra or the years/credentials.
3. **Generic tech name.** Calls it "Fractional (CO2) laser" — same as everyone. No protocol name.
4. **No question CTA.** "Check availability" is the most directive, lowest-converting CTA pattern.
5. **No deposit gate.** £25 consultation is fine; deposit reframe ("£25 reserves your slot, redeemed off treatment") is stronger.
6. **No date window.** "This month only" is vaguer than "Book by 31 May 2026."

## Reframe options for Eskeen (templates from the patterns above)

### Option A — Therapie-style evergreen (cold prospecting; lowest creative cost)

> Imagine looking ten years younger without surgery. 😍
> Putney's most-reviewed CO2 laser clinic — 137 five-star reviews.
> £395 covers full face + upper & lower eyes this month.
> **Ready to see what your skin could look like?**
> £25 consultation, redeemed off your treatment.

### Option B — Lavelle-style testimonial (proven 20-month pattern; needs a willing client)

> Sarah came into our Putney clinic this week for a CO2 fractional resurfacing consultation with Nurse Elanda. 👁️✨
>
> She opted for our full-face + upper-&-lower-eye protocol — virtually pain free, 5–7 days downtime, treats acne scarring, fine lines, hooded eyelids, sun damage and pore size in one session.

> This month, the full bundle is £395 (the eye treatment alone is normally £180).
>
> **DM 📲 for more info or book a £25 consultation online (redeemable off your treatment).**

### Option C — Lavelle-style date-windowed offer-stack (Eid/Summer-Glow style)

> **Summer Glow at Eskeen Clinic, Putney.**
>
> Full Face CO2 + Upper & Lower Eyes — £395 (the bundle is worth £575)
> Add half-price PRP enhancement — £50 instead of £100
>
> For bookings made **1st–31st May 2026** only.
> £25 deposit to secure your slot — fully redeemed off treatment.
> Open 7 days, including Sundays.

## Recommended testing structure

- **Cold prospecting (top-of-funnel):** Option A (evergreen, lowest creative cost) — set as the long-running ad
- **Engaged/lookalike audiences:** Option B (testimonial, needs willing patient) — refresh client name every 3 months
- **Retargeting (saw the page, didn't book):** Option C (date-windowed offer-stack) — refresh the date window monthly

Run all three concurrently. Don't kill the testimonial until it dies — Lavelle's has been live for 20 months.
